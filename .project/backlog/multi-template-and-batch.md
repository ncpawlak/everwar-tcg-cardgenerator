### Multiple templates & batch generation from a data file
**Added:** 2026-08-23
**Source:** User (via Planner, Gate 1 requirements)
**Context:** v1 targets a single frozen template (`Card_1.psd`) edited one card at
a time. Supporting other templates and bulk generation was deferred.
**Description:** Two related future capabilities:
- Support multiple/other card PSD templates (template selection, per-template
  editable-layer maps).
- Batch-generate many cards from a data source (CSV/JSON), rendering and exporting
  a PNG per row without manual editing. Likely pushes toward a Node render path.
**Priority:** Low (future)
