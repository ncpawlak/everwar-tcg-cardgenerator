### Artwork placement into the card art panel
**Added:** 2026-08-23
**Source:** User (via Planner, Gate 1 requirements)
**Context:** Surfaced while scoping v1 as text-only. The PSD has a white art panel
region; placing custom artwork into it was explicitly deferred.
**Description:** Let the user upload/select an image and place, scale, and position
it inside the card's white art panel, composited beneath the frame like the rest of
the baked art. Would require an editable image layer in the render pipeline (not
just text) and likely crop/fit controls.
**Priority:** Medium (targeted for v2)
